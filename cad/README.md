# CAD

Parametric bracket geometry modeled in Autodesk Fusion 360 and exported as STEP files:

- `bracket_baseline.step`: flat-plate baseline, 95 × 30 × 10 mm, two Ø6 mm chassis holes (x = 15 mm, y = ±7 mm), one Ø12 mm load hole (x = 80 mm, y = 0), 6 mm corner fillets.
- `bracket_channel_v2.step`: redesigned U-channel, 95 mm long, 40 mm wide web, 5 mm web and flanges, 15 mm flanges, 2 mm flange-web fillets, two Ø5 mm chassis holes and the same Ø12 mm load hole.

Both files are imported directly into ANSYS Mechanical (see `../fea_scripts/ANSYS_MECHANICAL_WORKFLOW.md`).
